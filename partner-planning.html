Agree the operating plan first

Decisions for Cory & Norman.

  • Confirm the pilot topic, date, duration, format, and admission price.
  • Agree teacher, moderator, production, and audience-building responsibilities and compensation.
  • Set any sponsor arrangement and its payment method; no founder split is currently agreed or modeled.
  • Choose account ownership, registration, payment, refund, and tax handling.
  • Confirm recording consent, replay access, and participant communication.
  • Approve setup scope, recurring work, advertising budget, and a realistic attendance goal.

Remaining cash does not automatically become a 50/50 or other founder distribution. Any such arrangement needs a separate agreement.
